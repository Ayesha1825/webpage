About Our College

Building strong academic foundations for students

Anjum Composite PU College is a pre-university college located in Davanagere, Karnataka.

The college offers 1st PUC and 2nd PUC education in three streams: Science, Commerce, and Arts.

The college focuses on academic learning, regular examination preparation, discipline, student development, and guidance for higher education.

Students interested in entrance examinations can also receive KCET and NEET coaching without any additional coaching fee.
